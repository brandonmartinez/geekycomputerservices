Leave It to the Geeks

Welcome to Geeky Computer Services. We are a newly founded business out of Ludington, Michigan. Specializing in computers and technology, we offer many different services to businesses, non-profit organization, and of course, the home user. Some of these services include web site design and setup, PC repair, Power Point slide show creation, document conversions, and more. If you would like to view the many different types of services we have to offer, visit the Catalog. If you would like know more about the different service categories and sub-categories, visit the Services Information section. There you will find information on the many benefits of the services offered.

Teacher Tech

Solutions for Educators

Geeky Computer Services is now offering lower prices and education specific services for educators. We want to help the schools get a grasp of technology even more than they have now. Offering things such as teacher web sites that will allow students to look up lesson plans (as well as help teachers manage them) and downloadable homework assignments. There will be many more services coming soon. In the catalog, services marked with "Teacher Tech" are included in this. These services are specifically meant for educators only!
